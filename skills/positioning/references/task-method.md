# positioning: task method

Load [gtm-context.md](../../shared/gtm-context.md) for scope resolution. Select the offering
(product, service, or solution), Motion, relevant Default/Custom Playbook,
and persona × segment cells. Honor supplied scope; ask only where an
ambiguous choice materially changes the work. List entities for discovery,
then get_entity for full selected entities and find_motion_icp with
learnings for selected cells. Follow linked capabilities/core features,
use cases, alternatives, triggers, objections, proof, and references.

Ground the strategy in field evidence with [Ask Octave first](../../shared/octave-research-toolkit.md#ask-octave-first-for-questions-that-span-records): ask `ask_octave` what wins the most deals and draws the most positive feedback for the selected offering, and which objections and competitors recur for the selected personas and segments. Use the answer to choose which cell learnings, findings, and calls to read in full, and cite the entities behind any field-evidence claim. Authored positioning still comes from the entities and cells; if the tool is unavailable, use findings and learnings directly and say so.

Build one strategy row per selected cell:
- Buyer situation, operating job, current alternative, and trigger to act.
- Consequence of staying put; buyer's desired outcome and success measure.
- Our capability → changed workflow → outcome mechanism.
- Differentiator relative to this alternative, prerequisites, and poor-fit
  conditions; distinguish advantages from table stakes.
- Committee roles: who experiences, champions, evaluates, funds, and vetoes.
- Applicable proof, scope/source, counterevidence, and unresolved claim.

Cover every audience the request names. When the request names two groups
("enablement and content leaders"), each gets its own cell or its own row
in the framework, matrix and committee map; fold one into another only when
the operator agrees, and say so in the document. A line saying an audience
has given no feedback, or that no proof exists for it, follows a search of
its calls, contacts and findings and names what was searched
([rule 26](../../shared/information-principles.md#claims-against-the-data)).
An operating estimate or typical figure ("about 15 minutes a week") keeps
its qualifier in the convincer, the pitch, the matrix and every other place
it appears (rule 29).

Derive the eight lenses from these rows. Explain the primary audience
choice; do not automatically prioritize User + Champion. Map the customer's
operating lifecycle separately from the buying journey. Homepage hierarchy
must match the requested scope: one focused offer, multiple entry paths,
or a platform narrative with audience routes. Do not impose one persona
on every company homepage. Preserve the same strategic claims across modes.

Load [evidence-and-inputs.md](../../shared/evidence-and-inputs.md) when material facts are missing. Ask
one grouped set of questions about unresolved buyer priorities, proof/POC
results, or capability constraints; incorporate and source the replies.
Keep the selected rows and open questions across turns. If answers remain
unknown, agree on qualitative copy, a working draft, or a measurement plan.

Only when persistence is requested, load [workspace-mutations.md](../../shared/workspace-mutations.md).
Propose exact changes to the selected entity/cell fields with before/after,
evidence, and expected effect. A narrow persona/section request does not
imply updating every persona × segment cell. Preserve other offerings,
Custom Playbooks, and learnings; verify intended changes by readback.
