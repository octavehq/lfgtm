# train: task method

### Teach the rep's actual remit
Read [gtm-context.md](../../shared/gtm-context.md) for offering/motion coverage, [evidence-and-inputs.md](../../shared/evidence-and-inputs.md) for examples and operator completion, and [output-readiness.md](../../shared/output-readiness.md) for lesson status. Load the slide-deck format, primer-sections, and lesson scaffold for interactive rendering; do not review this lesson as a scrolling HTML document.

Inventory products, services, solutions, relevant features, motions, segments, personas, and alternatives. Select coverage from the rep's remit and the customer's priorities. Retrieve library and relevant CRM/call examples first; ask a grouped clarification only if remit/priorities or necessary approved examples remain unknown. Continue supported lesson assembly and incorporate replies with provenance. The richest cell is a documented-content fallback, not evidence of commercial priority.

State what the lesson covers and excludes. Teach: what each relevant offering does and its limits; which buyer/use case it fits and does not fit; how acquisition, expansion, renewal, or displacement changes the argument; the relevant persona-specific problem, mechanism, proof, objection, and next decision; actual alternatives and legitimate tradeoffs. Use separate modules when offerings or motions differ materially. Preserve the six teaching jobs while allowing sufficient modules to cover the agreed remit.

Use zero to three approved real-customer archetypes according to available evidence. A reference record is not automatically a customer. Ask the operator for missing approved examples when useful; do not hold the rest of the lesson hostage to a count. If none exist, agree on clearly labeled illustrative buyer situations or omit the archetypes. Retain planned example placeholders only in an explicitly unfinished lesson draft.

Teach proof as outcomes customers have achieved, with offering/use case, baseline, period, source, and applicability. Contractual guarantees require their own approved basis. Never teach a reference result as a universal promise.

Checkpoint answers must be supported by taught content. Include scenario questions that require choosing the correct offering/buyer/proof, adapting to a different motion, or recognizing no-fit; explanations name the relevant decision rule. Keep first-attempt scoring as a learning aid, not sales certification. Store generation date, covered remit, and available source/cell revision identifiers in lesson metadata; an explicit refresh checks changed sources before claiming currency.
