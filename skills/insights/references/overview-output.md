# Insights overview

- Decision/question and scope: time window, filters, eligible population, unit, denominators, read/sampled/failed coverage.
- Findings ranked by relevance: observation, affected buyer/offering, evidence/source/date, counterevidence, limits, interpretation status.
- Trend comparisons: same filters and equal/comparable windows; unique events/accounts/deals, not excerpt counts.
- Messaging: separate seller usage, buyer engagement, concern resolution, and business outcome. Quotes preserve speaker and exact/paraphrase status.
- Current strategy match: full relevant library fields, applicability and whether a proposed gap is absent versus uninspected.
- Requested actions: owner, exact test/change, audience/channel, mechanism and validation event; preserve uncertainty.

Use [the task method](task-method.md). A narrow answer may omit unrelated sections. Do not fill a fixed number of findings or claim market prevalence from a retrieved sample.
