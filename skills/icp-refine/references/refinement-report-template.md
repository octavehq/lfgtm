# ICP refinement report

## Current scope

Workspace, offering/Motion/playbook and persona × segment cells; pursued, closed, won, lost and no-decision cohorts; time windows; exact outcome definitions; source and missingness. Segment attributes alone are not the full ICP.

## Evidence by candidate attribute

Attribute/value | pursued/eligible | wins/closed | comparable baseline | fit evidence | buying readiness | execution/commercial factors | uncertainty. Raw share of wins reflects pursuit mix and does not establish higher win probability.

## Explain outcomes

Keep fit, readiness, execution, commercials, product gaps and unknowns separate. Missing champion/budget/timeline is not automatically anti-ICP. Inspect source explanations and contrary examples before proposing an exclusion.

## Criterion design

For each proposed criterion: source/rationale, observable field or proxy, evidence requirement, polarity, must-have/weighted/exclusion status, applicable motion, false-positive/negative risk, and expected behavior under unknown evidence. Use only supported scoring semantics.

## Validation and application

Diagnostic versus held-out cases; routed/qualified outcomes; thresholds and unknown rate; subgroup/shared-agent regressions; sample limits. Show exact proposed changes, protected fields and verification/recovery state. Apply requested tested scope only after supported readback; never convert a report recommendation directly into an unverified batch write.

Use [the task method](task-method.md) for execution.
