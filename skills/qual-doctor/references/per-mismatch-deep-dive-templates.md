# Qualification mismatch

- Case identity, expected label and rationale; diagnostic or held-out.
- Configuration revision and actual routed entity.
- Returned raw answers, scores, confidence and sources.
- Per-question evidenceState: verified_yes, verified_no, unverified, conflicting, unavailable. Missing evidence is not verified No; retain backend raw answers separately.
- Returned candidate trace, or explicitly unverified routing hypothesis.
- One causal hypothesis and controlled test where supported.
- Proposed exact field change, affected shared agents, protected fields and recovery revision.
- Observed diagnostic/held-out outcomes, variance/regressions, verified readback and unresolved limitations.

Incumbent use may support displacement fit with a verified replacement trigger; the same incumbent may be excluded only by a relevant verified motion-specific constraint. Derive weights from the supported scoring contract and test results, never from a universal example.
