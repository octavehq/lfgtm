# deal-coach: task method

### Coaching focus from buyer evidence
Read [gtm-context.md](../../shared/gtm-context.md), [evidence-and-inputs.md](../../shared/evidence-and-inputs.md), and [output-readiness.md](../../shared/output-readiness.md) for the selected deal. Use the workspace's own methodology (the active Motion's, or one the user supplied) and follow [information principles #18](../../shared/information-principles.md#reading-calls-and-deals): R/E/C framing appears only when it is on file or the user asks for it, and with no methodology on file the plan carries no framework section. Load [analytics-contract.md](../../shared/analytics-contract.md) only for an actual benchmark or cohort comparison.

Resolve the opportunity and retrieve its current CRM stage, participants, commitments, and calls. Read a relevant persona cell for each material decision-maker. Ask grouped questions for missing recent decisions, commercial policy, POC results, or unrecorded constraints; continue supported coaching and incorporate the answers with provenance.

Before choosing goals, use [Ask Octave first](../../shared/octave-research-toolkit.md#ask-octave-first-for-questions-that-span-records): ask `ask_octave` how the selected account's calls and stakeholders connect to its objections and competitors, and what has worked on comparable won deals for the same persona and motion. Anchor on the opportunity and account ids and verify each claim with a narrow read before it shapes a visible line. Use the evidence list to decide which calls and persona cells to read in full. Comparable-deal patterns inform the coaching hypothesis; they do not set this buyer's readiness. If the tool is unavailable, proceed from the deal's own CRM and call evidence and say so.

Read every call in its setting first ([information principles #16 to #25](../../shared/information-principles.md#reading-calls-and-deals)). A committee gap counts only roles due at this stage, size and motion, and later roles read as needed later (#23). For the plan, an unanswered buyer pain ranks at least second among the goals, personal facts stay out of the plan, talk tracks and quiz, and a claim reaches a talk track or proof the champion may forward only once its record backs it.

Report three separate items: CRM stage; demonstrated buyer readiness; recommended coaching focus. Do not assign confidence percentages from fuzzy CRM-stage names. For each buyer readiness claim record the commitment or behavior, its source/date, contradictory evidence, and the remaining test. Confidence is supported, tentative, or insufficient evidence, with a reason; it is not a calculated probability.

Under R/E/C framing: Resonate concerns confirmation of a consequential problem and relevance; Elevate concerns the buying group's comparative case and decision criteria; Compel concerns an agreed reason/timing to act and a credible path through approval and execution. Pricing interest alone does not establish Compel. Different participants may have different unresolved decisions. An explicit stage flag chooses the coaching focus while preserving missing prerequisites.

Choose goals from the actual gaps, not a fixed quota. Each goal opens with what to do (a verb and the commitment it should win), then why now, then the evidence, each visually separate (#25), and contains recipient, desired commitment, relevant argument/proof, exact talk track, diagnostic question, and an observable success condition. Include a rehearsal based on the real objection and feedback grounded in the customer's method. A generic practice request without a deal does not trigger a routing loop to train.

### Diagnose before reframing
Treat objections as hypotheses. For price: distinguish unclear value, a genuine cash cap, unsupported ROI, scope mismatch, and commercial terms. For no problem: distinguish misunderstanding from a genuinely absent need. For delay: distinguish low priority, agreed timing, delivery burden, and approval dependencies. For competitor preference: identify the buyer criterion and any legitimate rival advantage.

Use CRM/call evidence first, then the next question that separates these explanations. Recommend supported value clarification, scope/tradeoff, approval work, recovery, nurture, or no-fit as appropriate. Do not invent concessions, features, or implementation commitments. If commercial terms are missing, ask the operator and keep those slots open in the working plan; incorporate the answer before final negotiation advice.
