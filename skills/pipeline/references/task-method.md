# pipeline: task method

### Resolve and diagnose the opportunity
Read [gtm-context.md](../../shared/gtm-context.md) and [evidence-and-inputs.md](../../shared/evidence-and-inputs.md) for this live-deal task; read [output-readiness.md](../../shared/output-readiness.md) before presenting recommendations. Load [analytics-contract.md](../../shared/analytics-contract.md) only for benchmark or cohort claims.

Resolve the account, opportunity, offering, and motion. Retrieve current CRM stage, owner, participants, commercial timing, and relevant calls before asking the operator for those facts. Use account history as context, but attribute decisive evidence to the selected opportunity. If identifiers or recent facts conflict, ask a grouped clarification and retain the operator's answer with provenance. Continue supported work while it is pending.

Keep current CRM stage separate from demonstrated buyer commitments and the coaching focus. For each plausible blocker record supporting evidence, contrary evidence, what remains unknown, and the next discriminating question or action. Recommend the move that most directly resolves the decision constraint, including wait, nurture, recovery, or no-fit when supported. Never infer inactivity from a narrow event window or assume every delay is a messaging failure.

Deliver: situation and desired decision; the leading diagnosis with alternatives; stakeholder-specific argument/proof; next actions with owner, condition/timing, and success criterion; usable draft wording or a concrete artifact request; open operator inputs. Incorporate replies and recheck the revised plan.
