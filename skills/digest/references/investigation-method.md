# Investigative digests

Use for new questions or comparisons, not every completed-report recap. Produce useful strategic understanding from available evidence. A directionally informative observation can justify a reversible test without proving causation or reaching statistical significance.

## Frame the decision and scope

Reuse the user's brief and established definitions. Identify the decision, audience, period, and primary questions; distinguish a question from the user's hypothesis about its answer. Ask only about unresolved points that materially affect the population, interpretation, or recommended action. Explain the uncertainty, why it matters, plausible choices, and your recommendation. Record the answer's scope. Do not require human approval for each analytical step.

Inspect business definitions before building the outline:

- Separate acquisition, expansion, retention, and other relevant motions from industry, segment, or persona. CRM labels, conversation intent, current customer status, and relationship at the time are distinct evidence.
- Use verified offering and capability hierarchies. A product mentioned in a call, parent of a mentioned capability, product sold, and product already owned are different relations. Preserve overlaps and unknown mappings.
- Historical ownership or customer relationship needs dated evidence. Current status cannot relabel an earlier landing; a same-day purchase may have unknown order. Do not invent workspace-specific rules for trials, services, reactivation, or account families.
- Use the product's canonical definitions where they answer the question and record justified departures. Reuse settled choices; when the user selects a link rule, do not replace it with a preferred stricter default. A stricter sensitivity population may still be useful and may itself be selection-biased.

## Inspect coverage before measuring

Use [research-tools.md](research-tools.md) to identify sources and actual capabilities. Distinguish structured CRM inventory, event activity, full conversation content, extracted findings, and authored GTM knowledge. Document each relevant layer's grain, time window, joins, completeness, and freshness.

For outcome comparisons, inspect coverage by group **and outcome** where supported. Missing recordings can correlate with losses. An event-filtered population cannot stand in for all eligible opportunities. Reconcile material business totals by record membership, not just scalar equality, including unlinked opportunities and exclusions. Local synced inventory also cannot establish records missing from its source mirror.

Keep unknown/unobserved, observed with no detected signal, and detected signal separate. A recording does not prove a question was asked. Processing completion is different from ingestion completeness. Do not display zero-filled time buckets as confirmed inactivity when coverage is unknown.

For bounded extracts, preserve request selectors/fields and pagination, reconcile received versus reported totals, and retain join keys. A top-N result is intentionally partial. Do not exhaust the entire workspace when a bounded aggregate and targeted evidence suffice.

## Measure the intended question

Create the compact claim packet and, for multiple comparisons, the ledger in [evidence-packet.md](evidence-packet.md).

- Define each arm, unit, numerator/denominator, date clock, outcome as-of, and linkage before interpreting a difference. Respect overlapping groups; they are not independent exclusive partitions.
- Use deterministic quantitative tools for counts. More objection mentions may reflect more calls; inspect distinct-account reach, observation volume, and within-group comparisons when supported. Do not divide mismatched tool totals to fabricate prevalence.
- Check plausible mix differences: record age/stage, field availability over time, collection behavior, channel/call type, and observation volume. Stratification or standardization needs explicit strata, common weights, and unsupported cells. Neither removes all confounding. Avoid treating outcome-dependent or post-exposure variables as neutral controls.
- For a claim about earlier exposure and later outcomes, validate event–deal linkage and temporal ordering. Current deal outcome plus an in-window call establishes association, not necessarily pre-close exposure. Date-only ties, sync-observed timestamps, reopenings, and insufficient follow-up need the tool's explicit eligibility/exclusion treatment. Do not infer ordering from a timestamp's apparent precision.
- For time-to-outcome questions, inspect maturation and open/right-censored records. An average among completed winners cannot describe all deals' progression speed.
- Inspect returned same-finding versus same-event semantics. An objection and an external speaker occurring somewhere in the same event do not establish that the buyer voiced that objection. Preserve event–deal pairs for stage/outcome statements.
- Read monetary definitions before summarizing. Keep currency basis, known zero, missing/conversion-missing, negative values, and amount eligibility visible in the method. A known non-negative subtotal is not total revenue. Negative values may be legitimate adjustments. Escalate unresolved business definitions for the affected monetary claim, not the whole digest.

Use available sensitivity results or repeat bounded comparisons across declared plausible definitions when ambiguity could change the story. Retain all variants, especially reversals. Do not select the definition that produces the desired result. Agreement across variants is robustness evidence, not independent replication.

## Explain without outrunning evidence

Use buyer language, distinct-account breadth, specific examples, counterexamples, graph relationships, and authored positioning to explore a mechanism. Preserve attribution: seller talk is not buyer sentiment; authored strategy is not observed demand. Inspect an independent source when a report repeats another report's conclusion; repetition is not corroboration.

A correct observed difference can be worth watching even with uncertain generalizability. State counts and scope and separate that observation from an inferential verdict. Undefined rates stay undefined. Inconclusive is not equivalent, and a nonsignificant result does not eliminate an explanation. Useful controls test specific alternative explanations; a flat placebo does not certify the entire analysis.

Choose a few valuable findings and explain what happened, to whom, and why it matters. Treat ordinary customer operating patterns as context, not automatic failure. Prefer a grounded message/targeting experiment over sweeping resource allocation when support is directional. A finding can be useful without an action panel.

## Compose and revise

Build the evidence table before the main conclusion and outline. Keep the requested questions visible, but combine overlapping sources into a coherent narrative. Retain material counterevidence and distinguish explored alternatives that were contradicted, inconclusive, unavailable, or not investigated.

Read the final title, chart, prose, and recommendation against the same claim packet. A cautious evidence note does not repair an overstated headline. After a source refresh or definition change, invalidate and recompute affected claims in every variant. When an audience saw a now-reversed conclusion, acknowledge the correction and explain the changed evidence or method. Do not silently replace it.

If one population or outcome is unusable, continue independently supported questions. Surface unresolved business choices early using the main skill's scope step; do not turn methodological uncertainty into a blanket refusal to deliver useful intelligence.
