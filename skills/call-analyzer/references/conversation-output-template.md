# Conversation analysis

1. Source and scope: transcript/email completeness, speakers and speaker confidence, date, account/opportunity, buyer committee, relevant offering/motion/cells. Parse provided content first.
2. What happened: buyer statements and commitments, seller statements, decisions and unanswered questions; keep exact quotes separate from paraphrases.
3. Eligible assessments: use the anchored scales and stage eligibility in [the task method](task-method.md). Show score, eligible denominator, source passages, counterevidence and reasoning. Use N/A for unobserved dimensions. Show configured qualification scores only if actually returned; no invented fit/100.
4. Evidence versus strategy: selected full library guidance, supported adherence or missed opportunity, genuine capability/fit limits and alternative explanations. Skeptical questions can show engagement.
5. Next steps: buyer-agreed commitments versus seller-proposed actions, owners, dates/conditions and success measures. Keep unconfirmed tasks unconfirmed.
6. Follow-up when requested: grounded response/message for the recipient, actual commitments, supplied operator corrections and remaining inputs. Recheck after replies; no generic mandatory email or new unsupported promise.
