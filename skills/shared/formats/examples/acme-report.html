Internal · 42 deals closed in Q3 2026 · written 11 October 2026

We won 11 of 42 Q3 deals; nine of the 11 raised pricing before the demo.

Most losses ended in Proof, where the buyer never named an owner for the pilot. The wins share a sequence rather than a segment, so the Q4 plan changes the order of the playbook and nothing about who we call.

What the wins share

Nine of the eleven wins put the pricing conversation before the demo, and the buyer set the scope of the pilot in the same call.

"Tell me what the full rollout costs before you show me anything, so I know what I am sizing the pilot against."
VP Operations, Acme Logistics · call of 12 August
"We put the pilot in the budget line the day after your pricing call; the demo was for my team, not for me."
Director of RevOps, an industrial distributor · call of 3 September

In the two wins that ran the usual order, demo first and pricing in the third meeting, the deal took 41 and 48 days longer than the median win of 52 days.

Across the 31 losses, 24 never had a pricing conversation on a recorded call. The eight internal-build losses all asked for a price in writing after the demo and got it two weeks later.

Deal size, segment and the rep's tenure do not separate wins from losses in this quarter's set; the sequence does.
