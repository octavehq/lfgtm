# Positioning for this meeting

Read [the meeting method](task-method.md). Match the offer’s mechanism and prerequisites to the buyer’s stated workflow and decision criteria. Compare the actual alternative with dated evidence and legitimate tradeoffs. Use available proof within its scope. Where account evidence is absent, pose a diagnostic question rather than asserting a pain or incumbent.

Use the selected customer methodology; select wording for each actual participant, not a universal objection rebuttal.
