# Strategy selection

Use the [meeting method](task-method.md) to choose complement, replacement, consolidation, build-versus-buy, or service positioning from this customer’s offering, motion and buyer evidence.

For each proposed recognition: established evidence → unresolved belief/decision → confirming and disconfirming question → consequence → conditional next commitment. A genuine limitation permits concession, recovery or no-fit. Never assume a context layer or multiplier. Separate seller rationale from wording the rep can say.
