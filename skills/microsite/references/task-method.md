# microsite: task method

Choose the route from the requested audience. For a named account, use its verified domain, CRM/calls, relevant offering, buyer and motion. For a persona/segment/industry page, use those library entities and representative evidence; do not invent a company domain or call company enrichment/qualification without a real account. Industry framing must connect a sourced industry constraint to the buyer's workflow, offering mechanism, relevant proof, and one action.

When a named-account page draws on calls, read them by [Reading Calls and Deals](../../shared/information-principles.md#reading-calls-and-deals) rules 16 to 21: the CTA matches the stage, buyer quotes appear only when suitable for the page's audience, and personal facts never appear.

Before publishing, check the page against two rules: every capability, security, data-handling or results statement traces to a product, proof point or reference record, and one with nothing on file is dropped or sent to the rep as a question (rule 21); the page shows no internal sources (coaching provenance, seller-side call labels, CRM amounts) and names only people in a record or on a call (rule 22).

Classify incumbent context as confirmed deployed, mentioned/evaluated, or hypothetical. State a deployed competitor only with supporting evidence. When unknown, use a fair current-workflow or category comparison; keep suspected vendor usage in internal research. Verify dated triggers and current competitor capability claims before using them externally. Never write that the target's “current solution likely cannot” do something as an account fact.

Resolve the intended audience before publishing: public marketing, recipient-restricted sales content, or internal strategy. Inspect the final claims, quotes, proof and downloadable files for that audience. Use restricted Octave hosting for private account material; public hosting is appropriate when it matches the user's intended distribution and the included material is suitable for it. Reuse an already authorized audience/host choice instead of asking again. Stage only the approved publish manifest. Verify the final URL, intended access mode, CTA destination, images and social metadata after deployment; review the final file again after any metadata/content rewrite.

When another skill passes approved content with renderOnly=true, preserve its finding/section/action hierarchy, evidence, brand, audience and privacy. Skip ABM strategy intake and do not add sales claims. Allow anchor navigation for long digests; the no-navigation rule applies to a short conversion-focused ABM page. Ask only for unresolved rendering/destination inputs and carry approval forward within its original scope.
